description: Learn the origin of Clay Robots.
---
Autobahn Schiene, founded toward the end of the 1800s was a little engineering outfit that got its start by manufacturing rail. By 2037, after a storied climb to the top that included dubious mergers and extraordinary operational thinking, they had gone international.

Trading under the ticker $ABR with a modest market cap of 10 billion, they came to be known as Autobahn Robot. Autobahn Robot was a throughline for humanity, connecting together some of the first cities in its Gilded Age to the very last.

As the population fell, robots under its employ were relieved of their duties and allowed to go on elsewhere. They had won their freedom in these circumstances.

Knowing only how to help, Clay Robots tried to save their creators, but they were lulled to sleep. At the point of no return, Clay Robots then relocated to Mesopotamia, the cradle of civilization and sought to replicate what once was and keep the spirit of humanity alive. They even towed remaining artifacts such as the Statue of Liberty into this machine city.

As stewards of the past, they kept alive the world, industry, the factories, and the cities, running just as they were as if people might return home.
